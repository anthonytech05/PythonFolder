About the Journalist

Meet Anthony

Anthony is an award-winning journalist with a passion for human-interest stories. With years of experience covering everything from Sunday community gatherings to week-long investigative series, he brings warmth, precision, and clarity to every piece he writes.

His work has been featured in national publications and community newsletters alike. Anthony believes that every story — no matter how small — carries the seed of something universal.
